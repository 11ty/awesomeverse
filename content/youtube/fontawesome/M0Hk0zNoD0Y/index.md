Did you know that Awesome has more than 30,000 icons in its core library? It’s true. And yet, not one of them is a ladder. We thought it’d be a good time to fix that.

In the first episode of Grid to Glyph, Font Awesome designer Noah Jacobus opens up Figma to build us a brand-new ladder icon from a blank 20×20 canvas, then carries it across every style in our Classic and Sharp families. Along the way, he walks through the rules that keep 30,000+ icons looking like they belong together, and highlights moments when breaking those rules makes an icon better.
