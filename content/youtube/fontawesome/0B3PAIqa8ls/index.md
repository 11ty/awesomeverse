Want to keep your projects loading up fast while still using the icons you love? In this screencast, we’ll show you how to subset your Font Awesome Kit, so you’re only loading the icons you actually need.

We’ll walk through the different ways to subset your icons—from automatic options to fully custom selections—so you can cut down file size, improve performance, and keep your setup streamlined.

🧰 What You’ll Learn:  
⚡ What subsetting is and why it improves performance  
🤖 The different subsetting options available in Kits  
🔎 How to create a custom subset by selecting specific icons  
💾 See how to enable 'Custom Subsetting' in your Kit settings  
🚀 How to use your subsetted Kit in your project
