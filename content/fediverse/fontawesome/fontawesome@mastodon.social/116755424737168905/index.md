We ran Build Week at Font Awesome, which was an open week to just build with AI and see what actually happens.

We were surprised that the tools amplified expertise instead of flattening it. The people who knew what they wanted used AI to go further in that direction.

Dave's take: "AI helps you be more of who you are, not less."
