Making a Kit now takes two steps, with a one-click Quick Start for anyone who wants to get going right away.

Making a new Kit got a lot shorter. Choose Quick Start and you're done in one click, or pick your styles and delivery method yourself in two steps. The package option moved to the final step, right where you'd look for it. Start one from [your Kits](https://fontawesome.com/kits).
