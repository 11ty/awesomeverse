We reworked subscription cancellation into a two-screen flow that shows exactly what changes at your renewal date, offers alternatives like switching plans or getting help, and asks a quick optional reason if you do decide to cancel. You'll find it under [billing in your account](https://fontawesome.com/account/billing).
