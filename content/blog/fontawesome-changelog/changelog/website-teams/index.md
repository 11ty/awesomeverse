Invite teammates to your account and give each one a role: owner, admin, editor, or billing.

Font Awesome is better with friends. Invite teammates from the [Team tab](https://fontawesome.com/account/team) in your account, and they get their own sign-in on your plan. No more passing one password around the office.

-   **Owners** have full control and can make another teammate an owner.
-   **Admins** can do everything an owner can, except make someone an owner.
-   **Editors** manage Kits and tokens, but not billing or the team.
-   **Billing** folks handle invoices and payments, and nothing else.
