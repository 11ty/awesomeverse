[Icon search](https://fontawesome.com/search) has been rebuilt around the thing you actually came for: finding the right icon, fast.

-   A rail down the side gives every pack its own place, so you can jump straight to Classic, Sharp, Duotone, or any of the Pro+ packs and see that pack on its own. Styles sit right above the results, one click away.
-   The grid is tighter and quicker to scan, with bigger icons, less space between them, and the actions you want on a cell (copy the markup, grab the SVG, drop it in a Kit) right there when you hover.
-   Filtered yourself into a corner? If your icon lives in a different pack, a different plan, or another category, you'll see it in its own section under the results, with a one-click way to go there.
-   When nothing matches at all, you get four ways forward instead of a dead end.
-   Search now reads for meaning, not just wording, so describing what you need in plain language can turn up icons that never use your exact words.
