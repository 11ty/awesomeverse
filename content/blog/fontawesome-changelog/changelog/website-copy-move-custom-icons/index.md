Copy or move custom icons from one Kit to another, one at a time or in bulk.

Custom icons aren't stuck in the Kit you uploaded them to anymore. Copy or move one from its detail view, or select a batch on the Custom Icons tab and move them all at once. No downloading and re-uploading required. Try it from [your Kits](https://fontawesome.com/kits).
