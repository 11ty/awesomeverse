Switch plans, billing cycles, or seat counts from the plans page, with a price preview before you commit.

Changing plans no longer sends you off to a separate billing portal. Start from the [plans page](https://fontawesome.com/plans). Upgrades start right away, and downgrades wait for your renewal date.
