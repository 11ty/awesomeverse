New icons, plus fixes for brand icons missing from Free and a few 7.3.0 icon glitches.

## Added

-   ##### **New Icons!**
    
    It's game on with new sports icons: [`padel`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/padel), [`padel-ball`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/padel-ball), [`cricket-bat`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/cricket-bat), [`field-hockey-stick`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/field-hockey-stick), and [`table-tennis-paddle`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/table-tennis-paddle).

## Fixed

-   These new brand icons were introduced in 7.3.0 but were mistakenly excluded from Font Awesome Free: [`visual-studio`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/visual-studio), [`dolibarr`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/dolibarr), [`obs-studio`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/obs-studio), [`storybook`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/storybook), [`a11y-project`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/a11y-project), [`copilot`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/copilot). Those responsible have been sacked, and the icons have been freed.
-   The new [`meerkat`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/meerkat) icon in 7.3.0 somehow got a capitalized name alias of "Suricata", though all names are supposed to be lowercase.
-   Some new Slab Duo icons looked already-pressed in 7.3.0, like their Slab Press Duo counterparts: [`box`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/box), [`bug`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/bug). They've been un-pressed.
-   The primary and secondary layers for the [`gear`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/gear) icon in Jelly Duo Regular were swapped.
-   These seal icons were redesigned and rotated: [`seal`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/seal), [`seal-exclamation`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/seal-exclamation), [`seal-question`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/seal-question).
