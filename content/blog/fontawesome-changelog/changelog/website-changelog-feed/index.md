You no longer have to remember to check back here. The changelog now comes to you, and it's easier for your tools to read too.

-   **Subscribe in any feed reader.** Every new release and website update now has an RSS feed you can follow. Add it to your feed reader, and new entries show up with their full notes and working links. Look for the Subscribe link under the months on this page.
-   **Readable by AI agents.** This page and our [license](https://fontawesome.com/license), [plans](https://fontawesome.com/plans), [privacy](https://fontawesome.com/privacy), and [refunds](https://fontawesome.com/refunds) pages are also published as markdown, so an assistant asked about Font Awesome gets the real answer instead of a blank page.
