Turn our marketing emails on or off from your account settings, without missing account emails.

Pick the emails you want from the Email Preferences section of your [account settings](https://fontawesome.com/account/settings). Unsubscribing from marketing emails never stops the important ones, like password resets and overage alerts, so you won't miss anything that matters.
