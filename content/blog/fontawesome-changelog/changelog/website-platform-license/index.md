A new Platform License lets the people using your CMS, page builder, app, or plugin pick and use Pro icons inside it.

Building a product where your own customers choose icons? This one's for you. Owners, admins, and billing contacts on a paid plan can request one from the [Platform License page](https://fontawesome.com/plans/platform).
