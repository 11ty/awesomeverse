Paid, lapsed, and team accounts can now be deleted from account settings, without contacting Support.

We'll be sad to see you go, but leaving shouldn't take a support ticket. Start from [account settings](https://fontawesome.com/account/settings), and we tell you exactly what goes with it first, including your teammates' accounts if you're a team owner.
