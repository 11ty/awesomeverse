A new Pro+ pack, Graphite, plus moon phase icons, zodiac symbols, new brands, and brighter sun icons.

## Added

-   ##### **New Pro+ icon pack: Graphite!**
    
    -   A brand new hand-drawn Pro+ icon pack from designer Vic Bell. Featuring over 200 of the most commonly used icons for app and website design, Graphite is a fantastic new additon to the Font Awesome family.

## Fixed

-   Adjusted the placement of the plus, minus, and xmark on our [`user-plus`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/user-plus), [`user-minus`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/user-minus), and [`user-xmark`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/user-xmark) icons to be more legible at smaller sizes.
-   The rails on our Duotone [`train-track`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/train-track) icons were mistakenly under the sleepers. We apologize to any trains that may have tried to use them. They've been fixed.
-   Some of the Sharp versions of our [`helmet-safety`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/helmet-safety) icon had round corners. No longer!

## Changed

-   **Here comes the sun.** Listen. Our old sun icons looked more like a gear than a sun. We're sorry about that. We updated all of our sun icons to shine a bit brighter: [`cloud-sun`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/cloud-sun), [`cloud-sun-rain`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/cloud-sun-rain), [`clouds-sun`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/clouds-sun), [`eclipse`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/eclipse), [`moon-over-sun`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/moon-over-sun), [`sun`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/sun), [`sun-bright`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/sun-bright), [`sun-cloud`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/sun-cloud), [`sun-dust`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/sun-dust), [`sun-haze`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/sun-haze), [`sunrise`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/sunrise), and [`sunset`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/sunset).
-   Reverted the [`chalkboard-user`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/chalkboard-user) icon to it's v6 design to better match the standard [`chalkboard`](https://fontawesome.com/icons/classic/solid/chalkboard) icon.
