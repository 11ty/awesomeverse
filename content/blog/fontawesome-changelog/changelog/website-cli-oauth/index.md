The `fa` command-line tool can now sign you in securely with the OAuth 2.0 device flow. Authorize it from your browser, with no tokens to copy and paste. Your clipboard deserves the day off. See the [Font Awesome packages docs](https://fontawesome.com/docs/web/setup/packages) to get set up.
