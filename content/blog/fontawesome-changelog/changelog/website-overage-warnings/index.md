Your account now shows a banner when Kit pageviews or bandwidth run over your plan, with a clearer breakdown on the billing page.

We've always let you know when your usage is heading past your plan's limits, and now you'll see it in your account too. Check the breakdown on your [billing page](https://fontawesome.com/account/billing). Surprises are for birthdays, not bills.
