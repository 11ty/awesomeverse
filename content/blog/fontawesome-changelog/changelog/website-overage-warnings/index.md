We've always let you know when your usage is heading past your plan's limits. Now those warnings show up right in your account too, with a banner when Kit pageviews or bandwidth start running over and a clearer breakdown on your [billing page](https://fontawesome.com/account/billing). Surprises are for birthdays, not bills.
