The Icon Wizard just learned a second color, and picked up a few other tricks along the way.

-   **Duotone icons.** Split your creation into two tones and watch it render like every other Duotone icon in the library.
-   **Multi-layer select.** Grab several layers at once and move or duplicate them together, instead of one at a time.
-   **Better controls.** Type an exact value and send any control back to its default with a single click.
-   **Nudge UI.** New arrows around the canvas shift the selected layer without reaching for the keyboard.
-   **Undo history.** Wandered down the wrong path? Step back through your changes until it looks right again.
