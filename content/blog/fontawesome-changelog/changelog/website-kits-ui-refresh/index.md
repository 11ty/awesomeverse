The Kit page is reorganized into three tabs, with settings and downloads in quick drawers and the embed code always in view.

We tidied the whole Kit page around what you use most. Five tabs are now three (Icons, Custom Icons, and Configure), settings and downloads tucked themselves into quick topbar drawers, and your Kit's embed code now sits above the tabs, always one copy away. Everything in its place, and not a single sock drawer in sight. Check it out on [your Kits](https://fontawesome.com/kits).
