We’re trying to make it easier for anyone to build, publish, and maintain web sites and today we’ve [launched a Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/fontawesome/build-awesome-pro?ref=43ttgb) with that goal in mind!

Where should you start?

-   Personally, I think the [**poster video on the Build Awesome Pro Kickstarter**](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/fontawesome/build-awesome-pro?ref=43ttgb) pairs nicely with any kind of cheese (but I just like cheese).
-   **Prefer a lower-level developer-focused introduction?** Read the blog post: [Collaborative Editing as Progressive Enhancement](https://www.11ty.dev/blog/collaborative-editing/).
-   Want to watch some more demos and videos? [Head over to the Kickstarter!](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/fontawesome/build-awesome-pro?ref=43ttgb) We have a few demo videos there!

We are so lucky to have the opportunity to build this project and _as always_ we are so grateful for your support!

_If you’re an 11ty Open Collective backer (active or otherwise), check your email for some secret tier rewards!_

[Thank you to the 838 backers of Build Awesome Pro!](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/fontawesome/build-awesome-pro?ref=43ttgb)
