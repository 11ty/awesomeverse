> “But I was going into Tosche Station to pick up some power converters!”

Until now, outgrowing one part of your Font Awesome plan meant upgrading the whole thing. If you ran out of Kits while your pageviews and bandwidth were fine as-is, the only fix was moving up a tier and paying for a pile of extras you’d never use.

That never felt right to us. If you love Solo and just need a few more Kits, you should be able to get a few more Kits.

Now you can. 

_(There’s no Uncle Owen blocking you now. You get those power converters, kid.)_ 

## Meet plan add-ons

Paid plans can now add extra capacity exactly where you need it, and nowhere else:

-   **Kits:** 5 Kits for $10
-   **Custom Icons:** 100 Custom Icons for $5
-   **Pageviews:** 250K pageviews/mo for $18/yr
-   **Bandwidth:** 1 GB/mo for $9/yr

Pick what you need from one screen and see your total before you confirm. Add-ons renew with your subscription, so there’s nothing extra to track.

![4 Font Awesome icons on a navy background: briefcase, pen nib, eye, pipe with valve](xXhlgKFNEL-1440-Bude7wuMa4aB.png)

## Cheaper than overages, on purpose

Overage billing is still there as a safety net if your traffic spikes unexpectedly. But if you already know you’ll need more pageviews or bandwidth, an add-on costs about 25% less than paying overages for the same usage. Unpredictable spikes can fall back on overages. Predictable growth is cheaper as an add-on. And add-ons are great for consistent billing, if you would rather have your bill be the same each month.

## Need a lot more?

Add-ons cover most everyday growth. If you need more than you can add yourself, reach out and our Actual Human™ Support team (ahem, Trevor) will help you find the right setup.

![4 Font Awesome icons on a navy background: upward chart on a monitor, plug plus, headset, control panel with sliders](S3QbCWsb8y-720-3dI0epcYtBOS.png)

## Check out your add-on options

Head to the [Billing section of your Account](https://fontawesome.com/account/billing) and then click the **Manage Add-ons** button to see what’s available for your plan. It takes about a minute, and you won’t have to switch anything.
