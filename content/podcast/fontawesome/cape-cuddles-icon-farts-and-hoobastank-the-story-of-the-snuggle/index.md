Every company has a name for the week everybody flies in. The offsite. The summit. The all-hands. At Font Awesome, it's called the Snuggle. For real.

Recorded from a hotel room at the October 2026 Snuggle, this episode digs into the Podcast Awesome vault to tell the story behind the most ridiculous name in company meetups. Matt stitches together clips from Dave Gandy, Travis Chase, Rob Madole, Jory Raphael, Cory LaViska, Ed Emanuel, and Frances Botsford. Along the way you'll hear how four guys and a beach house on Cape Cod turned into a fully remote company that still flies everyone in, and what keeps coming out of that week together. 

**What We Cover**

-   How the "Cape Cuddles" became the Snuggle, and why the name stuck 
-   Rob Madole on the real tradeoff of remote work: focus is great, but coworkers can start to feel like a resource
-   The Icon Wizard, a feature that got its start at a Snuggle
-   Cory LaViska's whirlwind trip from a Twitter follow to a porch phone call to Arkansas, plus the "Snuggle Is Real" shirt
-   How the Icon Wizard grew into something bigger, with Ed and Frances
-   Snacktivities: bite-size projects you can only really build in person (and a feature name that, thankfully, got changed)
-   What it felt like when COVID canceled the Snuggles
-   Rob's change of heart, and why he stopped being annoyed
-   Dave Gandy at the 2024 Snuggle, somewhere between karaoke and game night, on how loyalty gets built
-   Why the ridiculous name has one great side effect
