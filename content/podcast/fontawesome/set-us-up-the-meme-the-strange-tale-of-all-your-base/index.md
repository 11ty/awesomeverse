**Where did the “All Your Base Are Belong to Us” meme come from — and why did it break the early internet?**

In this nostalgic deep dive, Matt explores the wackadoodle origin of one of the internet’s most gloriously awkward memes. Before “going viral” was even a phrase, this phrase from the 1992 Sega Genesis game _Zero Wing_ somehow made its way from mistranslation to mass internet hysteria — with techno remixes, Flash videos, Photoshopped street signs, and even a nod from AOC herself.  
  

Yes, this was real. And it was spectacular.  
  

Whether you're an old-school web nerd or just want to understand why your coworker keeps saying “Set up us the bomb,” this one's for you.  
  

**🔹 What We Cover in This Episode:  
**🕹️ A breakdown of _Zero Wing’s_ hilariously mistranslated intro  
🎶 The techno remix and Flash video that made the meme explode  
🧠 Why “All Your Base” became the blueprint for internet weird  
📡 How meme culture has evolved from delightful chaos to... whatever’s happening now  
🧑‍🚀 AOC’s legendary tweet that brought it all full circle
